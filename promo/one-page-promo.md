# Stop guessing which pool quote is the right one.

### Free AI quote comparison — for serious pool buyers.

Pool projects go wrong in the **gap between the headline price and the contract**. Cheap quotes hide silent exclusions. Premium quotes look like overkill until you read the small print. Brochures and showroom visits can't tell you which company is actually solvent or which warranty will be honoured five years from now.

**Pool Buyer Magazine's new comparison tool reads your quotes the way an experienced project manager would — and emails you the analysis in about 10 minutes.**

---

## What you do

1. **Upload at least two quotes.** PDF or Word, straight from the builder.
2. **Tell us what matters most to you.** Pick two from *Cheap*, *Quick*, *Good*, *Cheap to run long term*, or *Low risk*. We weight the analysis to your priorities.
3. **Give us your email.** The comparison arrives in your inbox in around 10 minutes.

That's it. No phone calls, no sales follow-up, no broker referrals.

---

## What you get back

A full written report covering every quote, including:

- **Executive summary** — which option is genuinely stronger, in plain English.
- **Like-for-like adjusted price** — what each quote *really* costs once silent exclusions and provisional sums are normalised.
- **Scope integrity grading** — High / Medium / Low for excavation, drainage, electrical, plant room, commissioning and more.
- **Accountability chain** — who you'll actually rely on if something goes wrong: one company, or a chain of subcontractors?
- **Specification comparison** — pool shell, filtration, heating, sanitisation, cover, lighting, automation — line by line, with evidence level shown.
- **10-year ownership cost** — energy, chemicals, servicing, replacement cycles.
- **Companies House check** — legal status, incorporation, directors, ultimate owners, recent filings, outstanding charges. **Live data, direct from the official UK register.**
- **Review intelligence** — Google, Trustpilot, Houzz, Checkatrade and Facebook — separated into build, service and warranty themes.
- **Customer action checklist** — what to verify before you sign.
- **Bespoke questions to ask each contractor** — generated from the specific gaps and ambiguities in *your* quotes, not boilerplate.
